Tutorial
========

This tutorial introduces hotsos by walking you through installing the tool and
running your first analysis. By the end you will understand what hotsos produces
and how to read its output.

If you only need to look something up, the :doc:`../reference/index` and
:doc:`../how-to/index` sections may be more appropriate. To understand the
concepts behind the tool, see :doc:`../explanation/index`.
